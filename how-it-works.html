A simple daily routine

How IC works.

IC is designed to make the daily numbers easier to follow. Here is the proposed process, from sales to the next decision.

  1. 01

    Record sales and bills

    Bring your sales totals together and list what needs to be paid. The long-term goal is to connect these records automatically.

  2. 02

    Check the cash

    Review the starting drawer amount, cash sales, money paid in or out, and what needs to remain for change.

  3. 03

    Plan what is next

    Look ahead to expenses and identify which money decisions need attention first.

An example

Make sales numbers easier to understand

A $200 sales day does not necessarily mean there is $200 available to spend. Fees, taxes, and other deductions can change the picture.

IC is being planned to make that difference more visible.
